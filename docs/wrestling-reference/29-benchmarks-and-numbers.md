## 16. Quick sanity checks for a sim

If your simulation produces any of these, something is wrong:

- A promotion creating more than **3 new main-event stars a year** — historically almost
  unheard of
- **More than 40%** of a roster appearing meaningfully on a 2-hour show
- A performer working **250+ dates** in a modern era without elevated injury
- A title changing hands **more than 6–8 times a year** without prestige collapse
- A feud running **more than 5 matches** without decay
- A promotion with **no guaranteed revenue** surviving a bad year unchanged
- **Attendance above 90%** of capacity as a routine outcome
- A **brand split working** with fewer than 60 usable performers
- **Zero injuries** across a full roster over a year
- A gimmick staying at peak freshness for **more than two years** without evolution
